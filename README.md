# 🌿 HealthyHuman

**Empowering Longevity through Divine Nutrition**

Founded by **Mugagga M** (Technical Lead & Developer) and **Mawejje R** (Expert Human Nutritionist), HealthyHuman is a mission-driven platform dedicated to serving God by providing world-class nutritional knowledge and charitable support to those in need.

---

## 🌟 The Vision
We believe that the human body is a **temple**—a gift from God that requires diligent care and stewardship. Our mission is to provide the knowledge and tools necessary to maintain this gift, promoting longevity and vitality through proper nutrition.

## 🚀 Projects
- **Nutritional Knowledge Base:** Expert-verified data on foods and their impacts on human longevity.
- **Personal Intake Tracker:** A privacy-first tool to help users track their daily nutrition.
- **Charity Fundraising Portal:** A transparent platform to coordinate global nutritional aid.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** Plain HTML5 & CSS3 (Rapid Prototype)
- **Design:** Clean, modern, and inspiring visual identity.
